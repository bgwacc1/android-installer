How to Install (Android)
Step 1: Download the APK
Your admin will share the SynapseAi.apk file with you. Save it to your phone (Downloads folder).

Step 2: Allow Installation from Unknown Sources
Since this app is not from the Google Play Store, you need to allow your phone to install it:

Open Settings on your phone
Go to Security (or Privacy on some phones)
Enable Install unknown apps or Unknown sources
On newer Android phones (Android 8+): You may be prompted to allow this when you try to open the APK — just tap Allow
If asked which app can install unknown apps, select Files or Chrome (whichever you used to download the APK)
Step 3: Install the App
Open your Files app (or Downloads folder)
Tap on SynapseAi.apk
Tap Install when prompted
Wait for the installation to complete
Tap Open to launch the app
Step 4: Log In
The app will open showing the Synapse Ai login page
Enter your company email and password (same credentials you use on the web at https://synapse.biogreenwax.com)
Tap Sign In
You're all set!
How to Use the App
The Synapse Ai mobile app works exactly like the web version at https://synapse.biogreenwax.com. Everything you see on the website is available in the app.

Troubleshooting
"App not installed" error
Make sure you have enough storage space on your phone
Ensure "Install unknown apps" is enabled (see Step 2 above)
Try downloading the APK again — the file may have been corrupted during download
App shows a blank screen or error
Check your internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
Pull down to refresh the page
Close the app completely and reopen it
Forgot your password?
Contact your admin to reset your password
App feels slow
Make sure you have a stable internet connection
Close other apps running in the background
Restart the app
Uninstalling the App
Long-press the Synapse Ai icon on your home screen
Tap Uninstall or drag it to the "Uninstall" area
Confirm when prompted
